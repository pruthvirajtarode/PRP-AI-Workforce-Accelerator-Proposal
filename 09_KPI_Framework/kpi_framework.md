# Business KPI & Measurement Framework

*Note: This framework is designed to validate ROI after implementation. We do not claim guaranteed business results beforehand, but provide the structure to measure them accurately.*

## Measurement Model
The core of our measurement is the **Baseline → Target → Actual** model. Before training begins, we capture the baseline metrics. Post-training, we measure against our targets to calculate tangible ROI.

## 1. Program Metrics (Adoption & Competency)
- **Employee AI Competency Score:** Improvement from Pre-training assessment to Post-training assessment.
- **AI Adoption Rate:** Percentage of trained employees actively logging into approved AI tools weekly.
- **Active AI Users:** Total number of users sustaining AI usage 60 days post-training.
- **Number of Internal AI Use Cases:** Distinct workflows documented and adopted by departments.
- **Number of AI Prototypes/Workflows Created:** Capstone projects successfully moved into daily operation.

## 2. Productivity Metrics (Efficiency Gains)
- **Time Saved per Employee:** Self-reported and system-monitored hours saved per week on repetitive tasks.
- **Documents/Reports Automated:** Volume of internal documents generated entirely or mostly by AI.
- **Proposal Preparation Time:** Reduction in hours required to generate a CPaaS sales proposal.
- **Lead Processing Time:** Speed at which a raw lead is qualified and routed using AI assistance.

## 3. Customer Impact Metrics (Quality & Speed)
- **AI-Assisted Customer Responses:** Volume of support tickets closed utilizing AI-suggested replies or summarization.
- **Support Response Time (TTR):** Decrease in time-to-resolution for complex technical queries via knowledge assistants.
- **Campaign Iteration Speed:** Reduction in time required to launch A/B tested WhatsApp/SMS campaigns.

## AI Maturity Model Progression
We also track how the organization moves through the maturity model:
1. **LEVEL 1: AI AWARE** (Understanding concepts and risks)
2. **LEVEL 2: AI USER** (Using ChatGPT/Gemini for basic queries)
3. **LEVEL 3: AI POWER USER** (Using advanced prompting and context engineering daily)
4. **LEVEL 4: AI AUTOMATOR** (Linking AI to other tools for workflow automation)
5. **LEVEL 5: AI INNOVATOR** (Building custom RAG systems, agents, and scaling internally)
