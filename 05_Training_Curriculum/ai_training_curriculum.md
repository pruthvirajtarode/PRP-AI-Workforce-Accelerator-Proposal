# PRP AI Workforce Accelerator: Training Curriculum

This comprehensive curriculum is designed to move employees from basic AI awareness to practical application and, for technical teams, advanced innovation.

## MODULE 1: AI Fundamentals
- What is Artificial Intelligence?
- Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning vs. Generative AI
- Large Language Models (LLMs) and Natural Language Processing (NLP)
- Computer Vision and Conversational AI
- Introduction to AI Agents and Workflow Automation
- AI limitations, biases, and understanding "Hallucinations"

## MODULE 2: Generative AI Ecosystem
- Overview of major LLMs: ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, Microsoft Copilot
- Introduction to Prompt Engineering
- Context Engineering: Providing background and framing
- Requesting Structured Outputs
- AI-assisted research and synthesis
- AI-assisted writing, drafting, and editing
- AI-assisted data analysis

## MODULE 3: AI Productivity
Practical, everyday workflows for immediate time savings:
- Managing Email (drafting, summarizing threads)
- Meeting intelligence (transcription, summaries, action items)
- Documentation and SOP creation
- Automating internal reports
- Presentation outlining and structuring
- Proposal generation and customer communication
- Advanced Excel and data manipulation assistance

## MODULE 4: Advanced Prompt Engineering
- Zero-shot vs. Few-shot prompting
- Role and Persona prompting
- Chain-of-thought prompting (without exposing private business reasoning)
- Structured prompting techniques (Context, Constraints, Examples, Output formats)
- Evaluating and refining prompt outputs

## MODULE 5: AI for Sales (PRP Specific)
- Lead qualification and lead scoring logic assistance
- Deep customer and company research prior to calls
- Automated proposal generation based on CPaaS requirements
- Drafting personalized follow-up emails and WhatsApp messages
- Meeting summaries and requirement extraction
- Sales call analysis and objection handling strategies
- CRM hygiene and automated data entry assistance

## MODULE 6: AI for Customer Support (PRP Specific)
- Omnichannel conversation summarization (WhatsApp, Voice, SMS)
- Intent detection and Sentiment classification
- Generating suggested replies for complex technical queries
- Rapid FAQ generation from support transcripts
- Knowledge assistant querying for fast troubleshooting
- Ticket classification and automated escalation detection
- Building the Customer Support Copilot workflow

## MODULE 7: AI for Marketing (PRP Specific)
- Content generation for blogs, whitepapers, and digital marketing
- Campaign planning and audience segmentation ideation
- Designing high-converting WhatsApp campaign templates
- SMS campaign variations and A/B testing ideas
- Drafting interactive RCS content
- Social media content calendars
- SEO assistance and keyword optimization
- Campaign data analysis and personalization strategies

## MODULE 8: AI for Operations
- Document and contract parsing/automation
- Workflow automation for internal processes
- Internal knowledge base search and retrieval
- Data analysis on operational efficiency
- Automating SOP generation and updates
- General productivity assistants for administrative tasks

## MODULE 9: AI for HR
- Drafting precise, tech-focused Job Descriptions
- Resume screening assistance and key skill extraction
- Generating role-specific interview questions
- Creating internal Employee knowledge assistants (policies, benefits)
- Generating onboarding and training content
- Enhancing internal employee communication

## MODULE 10: AI for Management
- AI-assisted decision support and scenario modeling
- Generating executive summaries from long reports
- Strategic business analysis and competitor research
- Dashboard interpretation and anomaly detection
- Meeting intelligence for leadership syncs
- Automated KPI reporting

## MODULE 11: Advanced Generative AI (For Technical Employees)
- Working with APIs and LLM APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini)
- Understanding Embeddings and Vector Databases
- Implementing RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) architectures
- Function calling and Tool calling capabilities
- Designing AI agents and Agentic workflows
- Introduction to LangChain and LangGraph frameworks
- AI observability, testing, and system evaluation
- Implementing Guardrails for enterprise AI

## MODULE 12: Responsible AI & Governance
- Data privacy and protecting confidential PRP information
- Preventing prompt and data leakage
- Managing and mitigating hallucinations in production
- The necessity of "Human-in-the-loop" systems
- Copyright, compliance, and IP considerations
- Security, access control, and auditing
- Establishing Responsible AI adoption and internal governance policies
