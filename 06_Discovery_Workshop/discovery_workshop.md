# Discovery Workshop Agenda & Questionnaire

## Workshop Details
**Proposed Duration:** 45–60 minutes
**Attendees:** PRP Leadership, Department Heads, HR/Training Leads

## Agenda
1. Introductions & Aligning on Objectives (5 mins)
2. PRP Business Priorities & Market Landscape (10 mins)
3. Current Technology, Tools, and AI Usage (10 mins)
4. Identifying Department Pain Points & High-Value AI Use Cases (15 mins)
5. Training Expectations & Program Logistics (10 mins)
6. Success Metrics, Measurement, & Budget/Commercials (5 mins)
7. Next Steps & Summary (5 mins)

---

## Discovery Questions

### A. Business Context
1. What are PRP Services’ top 2-3 strategic priorities for the next 12-18 months?
2. How is the shift toward conversational AI (WhatsApp API, Voicebots) changing your clients' expectations?
3. Which of your services (e.g., Cloud Telephony, RCS, WhatsApp API) are driving the most growth, and where do you want employees to focus?

### B. Employees & Scope
4. Approximately how many employees are being considered for this AI training?
5. Which specific departments (e.g., Sales, Support, Engineering, Marketing) should participate?
6. Are there varied levels of technical literacy across these teams?

### C. Current AI Usage
7. What AI tools (if any) are employees currently using informally or formally?
8. Is ChatGPT, Google Gemini, or Microsoft Copilot already licensed or used internally?
9. Do you currently have policies regarding the use of public AI tools?

### D. Technology & Operations
10. What CRM, internal chat, and project management tools are currently in use?
11. Does PRP have structured internal documentation (SOPs, FAQs) that could support an internal RAG-based AI knowledge assistant?
12. Which internal workflows (e.g., manual reporting, lead qualification) consume the most employee time today?

### E. Training Logistics & Expectations
13. Do you prefer an intensive sprint (e.g., a 4-week program) or a phased transformation over 8-12 weeks?
14. What is the preferred delivery mode (Virtual Live, In-Person Workshops, Hybrid, Pre-recorded with Live Q&A)?
15. Do you want awareness training for everyone, productivity training for specific roles, technical training for developers, or a combination?
16. Is a formal certification process required at the end of the program?

### F. Security & Responsible AI
17. What internal company information is considered strictly confidential and must be kept out of public LLMs?
18. Are employees currently allowed to input client data or lead information into AI tools?

### G. Measurement & Success
19. What specific outcomes would make leadership consider this AI program a complete success?
20. Which repetitive tasks would PRP most like to see automated as a direct result of this training?
21. How do you currently measure employee productivity and time-to-resolution?

### H. Commercials & Implementation
22. Do you have a specific timeline for rolling out this enablement program?
23. Are you open to a modular commercial structure (e.g., starting with an Awareness Workshop and scaling up based on results)?
24. Are you looking to establish an internal "AI Champions" network to sustain this initiative post-training?
25. What is the approval process for finalizing a training and transformation engagement of this scope?
