# Company Research: PRP Services Private Limited

## Company Overview
- **Company Name:** PRP Services Private Limited
- **Industry:** Telecommunications / IT Services / CPaaS (Communication Platform as a Service)
- **Founded:** March 2013
- **Founders / Directors:** Rajneesh Dawar and Piyush Dawar
- **Headquarters:** 139, 3rd Floor, Avtar Enclave, Opposite Metro Pillar No. 233, Paschim Vihar, New Delhi - 110063
- **Current Company Positioning:** Communication Platform as a Service / Customer Engagement Platform

## Current Services & Business Context
PRP Services operates as a robust customer engagement platform designed to capture leads, automate journeys, and connect businesses with their audiences via omnichannel solutions.

1. **Bulk SMS:** High-volume text messaging for marketing, transactional alerts, and OTPs.
2. **Bulk Voice Call:** Automated outbound voice broadcasting for announcements and marketing.
3. **WhatsApp Business API:** Enterprise-grade WhatsApp integration for CRM, customer support, and automated marketing campaigns.
4. **RCS Messaging:** Rich Communication Services for interactive, multimedia-rich messaging experiences.
5. **Cloud Telephony:** Virtual numbers, toll-free numbers, and missed call services to manage inbound and outbound communications without physical infrastructure.
6. **IVR (Interactive Voice Response):** Automated telephony system that interacts with callers, gathers information, and routes calls to the appropriate recipient.
7. **AI Chatbot:** Automated conversational agents for websites and messaging apps to handle FAQs and initial customer queries.
8. **AI Voicebot:** Intelligent outbound and inbound voice automation that understands caller intent and reduces manual calling workloads.
9. **Lead Management System (LMS):** Software for tracking, managing, and qualifying inbound leads.
10. **Integration:** APIs to connect PRP communication tools seamlessly into existing CRMs and business systems.
11. **Digital Marketing:** Supplementary services to drive traffic and initial lead generation.

## Business Use Cases & Industries Served
PRP provides critical infrastructure for businesses needing scalable, reliable communication. Use cases range from simple OTP delivery and promotional blasts to complex, multi-step customer journeys involving WhatsApp CRM, AI intent detection, and automated escalation to human agents.

## Current AI Positioning
PRP is already integrating AI into its product suite, notably through AI Chatbots and AI Voicebots. The transition from rule-based automation to conversational AI indicates a strategic push toward intelligent customer engagement.
