# Department-Wise AI Opportunity Matrix

| Department | Current Challenge | AI Opportunity | Tool / Technology Focus | Expected Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sales** | Time spent manually researching leads and drafting CPaaS proposals. | Automated lead qualification and proposal generation. | CRM Integrations, Custom GPTs, Prompt Engineering | Reduced proposal turnaround time; higher lead engagement. |
| **Customer Support** | High volume of inbound queries; agents need time to understand context. | Conversation summarization, sentiment analysis, suggested replies. | NLP, LLM API integration, Support Copilots | Faster Time-to-Resolution (TTR); improved customer satisfaction. |
| **Marketing** | Iterating ad copy for diverse channels (WhatsApp, RCS, SMS). | Rapid generation of A/B test variations and campaign scripts. | Generative AI (Claude/Gemini), Context Engineering | Increased campaign throughput; highly personalized outreach. |
| **Product & Tech** | Documentation bottlenecks; scaling conversational logic for Voicebots. | RAG-based knowledge retrieval; AI Voicebot transcript analysis. | Vector Databases, Embeddings, LLM APIs | Faster product iteration; highly refined AI Voicebot interactions. |
| **Operations** | Repetitive manual reporting and SOP updates. | Workflow automation; document parsing and data extraction. | Automation Agents, Advanced Prompting | Significant reduction in administrative hours; improved data accuracy. |
| **HR** | Manual resume screening and generic onboarding materials. | Assisting with job descriptions, screening, and customized training content. | LLMs, Structured Prompting | Faster time-to-hire; improved employee onboarding experience. |
| **Management** | Digesting large volumes of departmental reports for decision-making. | Executive summarization, meeting intelligence, anomaly detection. | Meeting Assistants (Copilot/Otter), Data Analysis prompts | Faster strategic decision making; clearer visibility into KPIs. |
