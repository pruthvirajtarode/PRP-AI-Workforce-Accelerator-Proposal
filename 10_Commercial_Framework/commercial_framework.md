# Commercial Framework

*Note: Final pricing will be determined after the Discovery Workshop based on employee count, departments involved, chosen duration, delivery mode, customization level, and specific project requirements.*

## Base Training Options

### OPTION 1: AI Awareness Workshop
- **Scope:** 1-2 Day intensive workshop.
- **Focus:** High-level AI literacy, understanding Generative AI, security/privacy, and basic prompt engineering.
- **Best For:** Broad organizational alignment; introducing leadership to AI capabilities.
- **Trade-offs:** Great for quick alignment, but lacks deep hands-on practice and department-specific workflow automation.

### OPTION 2: AI Productivity Training (4-Week Program)
- **Scope:** 4-Week blended learning program.
- **Focus:** Advanced prompt engineering, daily productivity tools (email, meetings, reporting), and basic department use cases.
- **Best For:** Rapidly increasing general workforce productivity.
- **Trade-offs:** Highly actionable for daily tasks, but does not cover advanced, custom automation or building complex AI agents.

### OPTION 3: AI Workforce Accelerator (6-Week Program)
- **Scope:** 6-Week immersive program.
- **Focus:** Comprehensive curriculum including department-specific AI, introduction to automation, and hands-on capstone projects tailored to PRP.
- **Best For:** Deep departmental transformation and establishing internal AI champions.
- **Trade-offs:** Requires a moderate time commitment from employees, but yields tangible, custom-built workflows.

### OPTION 4: AI Transformation + Capstone (8-12 Week Program)
- **Scope:** 8-12 Week comprehensive enterprise transformation.
- **Focus:** End-to-end enablement, advanced Generative AI for technical teams, extensive Capstone development, and full ROI measurement.
- **Best For:** Complete organizational shift toward an AI-first operating model.
- **Trade-offs:** Highest investment in time and budget, yielding the highest long-term ROI and structural innovation.

## Optional Add-ons & Consulting Services
- **Pre & Post Assessments:** Formal evaluation and competency reporting.
- **Certification:** Official "PRP AI Champion" credentials for graduates.
- **Capstone Lab Facilitation:** Dedicated engineering/consulting support to build PRP's internal RAG/Agent systems.
- **Post-Training Support:** Monthly office hours, strategy check-ins, and prompt library updates.
- **Custom AI Implementation:** Direct consulting to build the AI Voicebot Improvement Lab or AI Sales Copilot.
