# Speaker Notes for Presentation Deck

*These notes accompany the 15-slide PowerPoint deck found in `03_Pitch_Deck`.*

## SLIDE 1: Title Slide
**Notes:** Welcome the PRP leadership team. State the purpose of the meeting: to discuss how we can move PRP from simply having AI technology in its products, to having a workforce that natively understands and leverages AI to drive productivity and innovation.

## SLIDE 2: Executive Summary
**Notes:** Highlight that tools like ChatGPT are easy to buy, but hard to use effectively at an enterprise scale without risking data privacy or wasting time. The goal is to build *capability*, not just grant access.

## SLIDE 3: Understanding PRP Services
**Notes:** Briefly reflect back our understanding of PRP. Acknowledge your success in CPaaS, SMS, WhatsApp API, and Voice. This builds trust and shows we aren't delivering a generic pitch.

## SLIDE 4: PRP's Existing AI & Ecosystem
**Notes:** Point out that PRP already knows AI—you build Voicebots and Chatbots. But does your HR team know how to use AI? Does your sales team use it to write proposals? This slide bridges external product with internal operations.

## SLIDE 5: Why Workforce AI Enablement Matters
**Notes:** Focus on the "Productivity Multiplier." Share that without training, employees use AI for trivial tasks. With training, they automate hours of reporting and lead processing every week.

## SLIDE 6: AI Opportunity Map
**Notes:** Walk through the matrix. Show how Sales and Support usually see the fastest ROI (High Impact/Low Effort), while Engineering and Product require more advanced enablement but yield massive strategic value.

## SLIDE 7: Proposed AI Workforce Accelerator
**Notes:** Introduce the core program. Emphasize that it is customized. It is built for a CPaaS company, not a generic retail or finance business.

## SLIDE 8: Learning Architecture
**Notes:** Address the common fear of boring corporate training. Emphasize the 40% hands-on practice. If they don't build something, they won't remember it.

## SLIDE 9: Department-Specific AI
**Notes:** Pick 2 examples to highlight. For instance, how Sales can use an LLM to instantly summarize a 30-minute discovery call transcript and generate a custom WhatsApp API proposal.

## SLIDE 10: Hands-on PRP AI Projects
**Notes:** Explain the Capstone concept. We aren't just teaching them; we are guiding them to build internal tools—like an AI Knowledge Assistant for your own APIs—that they will use the day after training ends.

## SLIDE 11: Learning & Assessment Model
**Notes:** Explain how we prove it worked. We measure their knowledge before we start, and after we finish, giving leadership a clear competency report.

## SLIDE 12: AI Maturity Journey
**Notes:** Explain that not everyone needs to be a Level 5 Innovator. We want HR to be Level 3 Power Users, while your tech leads should reach Level 5.

## SLIDE 13: Implementation Roadmap
**Notes:** Walk through the timeline. Highlight the "AI Champions" concept—we want to train internal leaders who will sustain the momentum long after our engagement ends.

## SLIDE 14: Success Measurement
**Notes:** Emphasize that we measure what matters to the business: time saved, proposals generated, and tickets resolved. We provide the framework to calculate real ROI.

## SLIDE 15: Discovery & Next Steps
**Notes:** Open the floor for the Discovery Workshop. Transition smoothly into asking the first questions from our Discovery Questionnaire.
